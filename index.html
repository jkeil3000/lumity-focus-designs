A thoughtful pause before the scroll.

Lumity Focus interrupts automatic app opening with a short passage, video, or direct action, then lets the user return to their day or unlock only the requested app for a limited session.

Intentional frictionA pause that creates a real decision without becoming punishment.

Finite, curated contentExternally sourced passages and clips rotate without replacement.

Core screens

These screens describe the intended hierarchy, copy density, visual system, and interaction sequence. Use the interactive prototype to test navigation and limit behavior.

01 · WelcomeShort introduction with one clear setup action.
02 · Screen Time accessRequired authorization explained plainly.
03 · Choose appsNative app selection during setup.
04 · Home and limitsIndividual or group rules with per-open and daily allowances.
05 · Blocked appShows today’s opens and directs the user into Focus.
06 · Choose a directionRead or watch, do something, or choose Surprise me.
07 · PassageAttributed source text with deliberate line breaks and optional reflection.
08 · ResetOne direct instruction with an accessibility alternative.
09 · RelationshipsA concrete action without added coaching or motivational setup.
10 · MovementA simple physical action with a lower-impact alternative.

Build the native proof before the full interface.

The HTML is a design and interaction specification. The first engineering milestone should validate the core Screen Time behavior on a physical iPhone.

  • Carry the triggering app token from the shield into Lumity Focus.
  • Unlock only that app while every other selected app stays blocked.
  • Enforce the configured session across backgrounding, force quit, and reboot.
  • Persist app and group usage without resetting the daily allowance.
